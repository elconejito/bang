TOGGLES Per line, three switches: Deduct = ON (pick the load), Firearm count = ON, Suppressor count = ON when a can is mounted. A mounted can shows a swap › to log a different one just for the trip (Line 1). Toggle it on when none is mounted (Line 2) and a picker appears to attach an existing can or add a new one — so it still earns a round count. Every preview shows before → after.
WHY OFF Turn Deduct off for ammo you don't track (borrowed, range-provided) or dry/training without live fire; turn firearm count off for a friend's gun. The toggles keep the log honest without forcing data you don't have.
