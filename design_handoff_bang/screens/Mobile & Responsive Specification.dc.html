Visible state marker
Loading, Empty, and Error states preserve the same mobile hierarchy; retry and recovery actions remain reachable at 375PX.
Breakpoints
Desktop ≥1024: persistent app bar, 32px gutters, dense tables. Tablet 600–1023: 24px gutters, 2-up cards. Mobile 375–599: 16px gutters, one column, bottom navigation.
Priority & overflow
Preserve identity, current count/status, and next action. Move secondary metadata behind Details. Tables become stacked labeled rows or retain horizontal scroll with a visible cue—never truncate essential values.
Touch & a11y
All tap targets are ≥44px; bottom nav has named controls and current-page state. Sticky actions respect safe-area inset. Zoom up to 200% must reflow without loss; keyboard focus remains visible in drawers and dialogs.
